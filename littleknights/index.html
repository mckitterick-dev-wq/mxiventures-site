Our Philosophy

A stimulating place to grow, built around each child.

Little Knights offers children the opportunity to develop physically, socially, and cognitively, according to their own learning style. We consider each child unique and adjust to their temperament and rate of development, because kids learn best when they are having fun.

A loving, home-like environment

A licensed center with the warmth and calm of an in-home daycare, where children feel safe enough to explore.

Curriculum tuned to the child

Lessons are planned to enhance and challenge each child's needs, interests, and abilities, with hands-on activities every day.

Qualified, familiar faces

Every classroom has at least one teacher with an Early Childhood degree, and many families stay with us from infancy through preschool.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

What are your hours?
We are open Monday through Friday, 6:30am to 6:00pm. We stay open later than most centers in the area, which gives parents who work further away time to get through evening traffic.
How big is your program?
We have six classrooms and are licensed for 60 children. Squires (infants) has 5 children with 2 teachers, Explorers has 10 with 3, Vikings has 11 with 2, Crusaders has 12 with 2, Jr Apprentice (preschool) has 12 with 2, and Knights (school age) has 10 with 1.
Why is the waitlist so long?
The average wait is about 15 months. Our class sizes are small and our turnover is very low. Most children stay from Infant through Preschool. When a family adds another child, that child goes to the top of the list, since we like to keep families together at the same center.
How do school-age children get to Little Knights?
Alton School District buses students from Lewis and Clark, North, West, and Gilson Brown Elementary. Our summer program is only for school-age children already enrolled during the school year, and our preschool graduates get first spots as openings come up.
What qualifications does your staff have?
All staff have current CPR and First Aid training, and all lead teachers have a degree in Early Childhood. The Director holds a Masters in Early Childhood and is Car Seat Certified, so we can help with every child's needs, whether Infant, Toddler, Preschool, or School Age.
How do I schedule a tour?
Tours are given Monday to Friday, 8:45am to 10:30am and 2:30pm to 5:00pm, by appointment only. We cannot accommodate tours during lunch or naptime. Fill out the form below or call 618-208-0426.
