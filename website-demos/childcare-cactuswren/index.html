Our approach

Built around the child in front of us.

Children arrive at different speeds and leave at different speeds. We plan a week around what a room is actually ready for, then let the day move when it needs to. The aim is not to get ahead of anyone. It is to be somewhere a child is happy to be dropped off.

A room that feels like a room

Low shelves, real materials and enough floor to spread out on. Nothing important is kept behind glass.

The same teacher all year

Rooms are staffed by people who stay. Your child is known by name in the first week, not the first term.

Told what actually happened

A short note at pickup about the day, not a printed form. If something went wrong you hear it from us first.

Inside the building

What a Tuesday looks like.

Doors at 6:30 and breakfast until 8. Each room runs its own morning, then everyone is outside before it gets hot. Lunch, nap, and a slower afternoon until the last pickup at 6.

We are closed on the six federal holidays and for two days of staff training each August. Those dates go out in January so nobody is caught out.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What are your hours?
Monday to Friday, 6:30am to 6:00pm. Part days are available in every room except the nursery, which is full days only so the schedule stays steady for the babies.
How big are the rooms?
We are licensed for 60 children across six rooms. The nursery runs five children to two teachers. The older rooms run larger and are staffed at the state ratio or better, and we post the current ratio on each room door.
Do you have openings?
It moves month to month. Usually about half the rooms have space and half have a short list. Call and ask what is open now rather than joining a list you may not need.
What does it cost?
Rates depend on the room and on full or part days. We will quote you over the phone in about two minutes, before you come and look at anything.
What do we need to send?
A change of clothes and whatever they sleep with. Meals, snacks, wipes, sunscreen and hats are included.
How do I visit?
Walk in between 9 and 11 or 3 and 5 on a weekday, or send the form below and we will call you back the same day.
