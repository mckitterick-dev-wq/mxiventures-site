Privacy policy

Last updated 11 August 2026

This page describes what happens to the information you give this website, and what this website does not do. It is written to be read, not to be scrolled past.

What you give us

The contact form on this site asks for four things: your name, your phone number, your email address, and whatever you choose to type in the message box. Nothing on this site asks for anything else, and there is nowhere on it to create an account.

Where it goes

When you send the form, it goes to Ironwood Heating & Cooling as an email. The reply address on that email is the one you typed, so a reply comes back to you directly. It is not added to a mailing list, it is not put into an advertising system, and it is not passed to anyone outside the business.

Your phone number

If you give us a phone number, it is so we can answer you. By sending the form you agree we may contact you about your inquiry. We do not sell or share your details. If you would rather we only used email, say so in the message and we will.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not set cookies and does not track you. There is no analytics, no advertising pixel, and no session that follows you from page to page. If you come back tomorrow, this site does not know that you were here today. The one exception to be honest about is the Google Map above: it is Google's frame, and what Google sets inside it is Google's, not ours.

What this site loads from other companies

Two things on this site come from somewhere other than our own server, and both can see your IP address when they load:

  • A Google Map on the contact section, showing where we are. It is provided by Google and loads when you scroll to it. Google's own privacy policy applies to it.
  • The company that hosts this website and delivers it to your browser. It records standard server request information for security and reliability.

Everything else is served from this site directly: the type, the images, the styling and the scripts. There is no font service, no content network and no advertising script. That matters for a reason that is easy to miss, and it is why the map is listed above rather than left unmentioned: anything a page loads from an outside company tells that company you visited, even if you never click it.

What the host records

Those request records are the ordinary kind every web server keeps: an address the request came from, the time, the page asked for, and which browser asked. They exist to serve the site and keep it working, they are held briefly, and they are not used to build a profile of anyone.

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your details.
  • Share them with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Send you anything you did not ask for.

Asking what we hold, or asking us to delete it

Call (602) 555-0100 or email hello@ironwood.example and ask. If you want the message you sent deleted, say so and it is deleted.

If this changes

If the site starts doing something this page does not describe, this page is updated before that change goes live, and the date at the top changes with it.
