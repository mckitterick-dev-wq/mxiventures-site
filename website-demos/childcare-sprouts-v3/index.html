THE LITTLE SPROUTS WAY

Quiet, consistent
excellence.

We aren't a franchise. We don't have an app that tracks every diaper change. We believe that true early learning requires deep, unbroken attention from an adult who knows your child intimately.

By capping our rooms at six to eight children and keeping the same lead teacher in that room for the entire academic year, we create a sanctuary of predictability.
