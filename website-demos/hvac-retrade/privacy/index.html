Privacy

What this site collects, and what happens to it.

This is a demonstration site for a business that does not exist. Nothing sent through the form on it reaches anyone, and no enquiry is stored. The disclosure below is the one a real build ships, shown so you can read it before you buy one.

What the form collects

Name, phone number, email address, and whatever you type in the message box. Nothing else, and there are no hidden fields.

What it is used for

Answering the enquiry, and nothing else. Details are never sold, rented or shared with anyone for marketing. On a live build the message goes to the owner's own mailbox and the reply-to is set to the visitor, so the owner answers from the mail app they already use.

What this page loads from other companies

Nothing. Fonts are served from this site, not from a font host. There is no analytics, no advertising pixel, no chat widget, no cookie banner — because nothing here sets a cookie or tracks anyone. A banner with nothing to consent to is theatre.

A live build may carry one exception: a keyless Google Maps embed on the contact section, which loads from Google and is disclosed here by name when it is present. This demonstration page does not carry one.

Phone numbers

Giving a phone number on the form means agreeing to be contacted about that enquiry. It does not opt anyone into marketing messages, and there is no list to be added to.

Getting it removed

On a live build, ask the owner and it is deleted. There is no account to close.

This page describes what this website does. It is not legal advice, and a real business should have its own counsel confirm its obligations.
