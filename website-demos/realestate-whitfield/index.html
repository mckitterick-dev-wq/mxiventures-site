About Dana

A solo practice by design.

For two decades, I have chosen to remain a solo agent. In an industry obsessed with scale and "teams," I believe that representing significant properties requires a singular, dedicated focus. When you hire me, you work with me—from the initial consultation to the final signature.

My understanding of Central Phoenix isn't derived from an algorithm; it's built on twenty years of living, working, and observing the subtle shifts in our historic neighborhoods.

You've seen the postcards. You know the market is shifting. But you aren't a transaction. Let's talk about the history of your home before we talk about the price.

Selling a home in historic Phoenix requires more than a standard MLS listing. It requires understanding the nuances of the neighborhood, the architectural significance of the property, and the specific needs of a discerning buyer. My approach is intentionally slow, highly protective of your privacy, and focused entirely on presenting your home as the architectural asset it is.
